BACKGROUND Place before prediction

A geographer’s
way of looking.

Before data science, I studied Geography. That background still shapes the questions I ask: where was this measured, at what scale, and what disappears when the real world becomes a table?

My undergraduate research took me to the River Soar to examine riparian buffers, land use and nutrient conditions. Today, my work ranges from Census movement patterns to football forecasts and individual trees in laser scans.

Across these projects, I care about making the route from source data to result clear enough for someone else to inspect. The code, evaluation choices and limitations belong alongside the findings.
