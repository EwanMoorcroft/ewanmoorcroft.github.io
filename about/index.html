From Geography to data science

I began with a BSc in Geography at Liverpool. Field research on the River Soar involved examining land use, riparian buffers and nutrient conditions. That experience still shapes my approach to data: understand how it was collected before deciding what a model can tell you.

What I build

My projects combine data preparation, model evaluation and usable software interfaces, including command-line tools, automated checks and a local API. I work mainly in Python, with SQL and R for data processing and statistical analysis. I’m particularly interested in the connection between modelling and software: making inputs reliable and results useful to someone else.

Experience working in teams

Alongside my studies, I have worked in food production, markets and warehouse operations. At Fosse Meadows I worked in a two-person market team, serving customers, handling payments and preparing orders to same-day deadlines. These roles gave me practical experience of coordinating work, communicating with customers and following through on responsibilities.
