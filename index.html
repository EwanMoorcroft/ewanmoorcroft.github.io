From Geography to data science

My BSc Geography dissertation combined water sampling, laboratory nutrient measurements, Python analysis and QGIS mapping on the River Soar. Geographic Data Science coursework took this further through raster and vector mapping, spatial statistics and computational reports. That background connects my interest in the physical world with the software I now build.

What I build

I work mainly in Python, with SQL and R for data processing and analysis. My MSc has extended this into machine learning, databases and web applications. Coursework includes a text-clustering command-line tool and a PHP/MySQL booking application; my larger projects bring data preparation, model evaluation and usable interfaces together.

Experience working in teams

At Fosse Meadows I worked in a two-person market team, serving customers, handling payments and preparing orders to same-day deadlines. I have also worked in warehouse operations. These roles involved coordinating with colleagues, communicating clearly and following through on practical responsibilities.
